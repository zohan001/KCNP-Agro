## SLIDE 11 — The Value Chain: What Middlemen Actually Do

**Title:** We Don't Remove the Middleman — We Remove His Monopoly

**Sub:** Who sits between the coastal farmer and the buyer, and which of their jobs a platform
can and cannot do.

**Body (three columns):**

| 1. The chain | 2. What we digitise ✅ | 3. What we partner on 🤝 |
|---|---|---|
| Farm gate → broker → aggregator → transport → Kongowea → wholesaler → retailer/hotel/export | Price discovery (Price Board) | Aggregation & bulking |
| Broker provides: buying, **cash advance**, transport, grading | Matching & offers (multiple buyers bid) | Transport & cold chain |
| Farmer sells spot at the gate, no alternative buyer, no price knowledge | Reputation & verification | Storage & grading |
| Result: 80–95% of smallholder mango goes through middlemen; farmer captures **<30%** of final value | Traceability & trade records | **Working capital / advance credit** |

**Bottom strip:** "Verified, rated aggregator partners — visible and accountable, not invisible."

**Speaker note (30s):**
> "Kenya's middleman is not a fee — he's the farmer's transport, grader and credit line. That's
> why 40 platforms that tried to delete him failed. We only do what software does well:
> information, matching and trust. The rest, we partner with and make accountable."

## SLIDE 12 — Why This Time Is Different (Our Assumptions, Tested)

**Title:** We Know What We Don't Know Yet

**Sub:** The four assumptions we are testing before scaling — and the features that prove them.

| Assumption | Status | What proves it |
|---|---|---|
| Farmers want a digital selling channel | Unproven — only 8% of Kenyan farmers use digital marketplaces `[60 Decibels/Gates 2024]` | Demand Board + interviews |
| We can replace middleman functions | **No — we partner** | Verified-aggregator model |
| Farmers will pay a subscription | Unproven | Free-to-list, pay after first trade |
| Buyers will buy direct | Unproven | Offers/Quotes + escrow pilot |

**Bottom strip:** "We report **trades**, not listings — because that's the number that matters."

**Speaker note (30s):**
> "We've audited our own assumptions instead of hiding them. Our edge isn't a promise to
> abolish the trader — it's transparency, choice and verified partners, proven with real trade
> data."
