## 1. The one-paragraph answer to the judges' homework

You asked what assumptions we are making and how middlemen actually work. We audited both.
**The finding that changes our strategy:** the middleman is not an exploitative fee — he is the
farmer's aggregator, transporter, quality-grader **and, most importantly, their source of
advance cash**. Kenya-specific evidence shows **80–95% of smallholder mango moves through
middlemen**, who add only ~8% of value but earn ~22% gross margin, while farmers who sell spot
at the gate capture **under 30% of the final value** and cannot access credit elsewhere. That is
why projects that promise to "eliminate the middleman" fail: they remove the farmer's buyer,
logistics and credit and replace them with a website. **KCNP Agro does not pretend to delete
those functions. We digitise the one layer software is genuinely good at — information,
matching, reputation and traceability — and we make the rest accountable through verified,
rated aggregator partners.**

## 2. What the evidence says (with sources)

| Claim | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Farmers lose value to the chain | Mango: 80–95% sold via middlemen; aggregators +8% value / 22% margin; farmer adds ~22% of chain value `[S1]`. Farmers capture **<30%** of final value; 85% attribute post-harvest loss to **market** causes `[S3]`. |
| Middlemen provide real services | Aggregation, transport, grading, storage, and **pre-harvest advance credit** `[S13]`,`[S14]`,`[S15]`; farmers sell at the gate specifically to avoid transport + market-search costs `[S3]`. |
| "Just sell direct online" doesn't work alone | M-Farm (Safaricom-backed): *"the root problem was not pricing transparency but low volume and buyers' hassle"* `[S24]`. Only **8%** of Kenyan farmers used any digital marketplace to sell, with **weak demand** for selling tools `[S11]`. |
| Owning logistics is brutal | Twiga ($150M+ raised, own fleet + 200,000 sq ft hub) cut ~40% of staff, suspended Nairobi ops and **pivoted from fresh produce to FMCG** `[S23]`. |
| The device reality | Only **53% of rural Kenyans own an internet-capable phone**; ~30M feature phones still in use; iCow reached 1.6M farmers mainly via USSD/SMS `[S20]`,`[S18]`,`[S25]`. |
| Payment reality | Mobile money is near-universal (92.8%) but farmers often **prefer cash** because M-Pesa carries a fee `[S12]`; subscription fees are a named affordability barrier `[S30]`. |
| The market is real and growing | Kenya digital-ag market ~**USD 1.2 bn (2025) → 2.5 bn (2031)**, ~17% CAGR. |
| Meat of the coastal basket | Mango: coast+Eastern ≈90% of national output. Cashew: low farm-gate prices are killing acreage. Coconut: ~KES 12.2 bn, largely informal `[S1]`,`[S7]`,`[S9]`. |

## 3. Our five load-bearing assumptions (and what we're doing about them)

| Assumption | Status | Action (30 days) |
|---|---|---|
| Farmers want a digital selling channel | **Unproven** (8% use) | Interview 30 farmers; test demand board |
| Platform can replace middleman functions | **False as stated** | Partner model: verified aggregators + demand-led aggregation |
| Farmers will pay KSh 800/month | **Unproven** | Price test after a real trade; free-to-list → pay after first sale |
| Buyers will buy direct from smallholders | **Unproven** | Ask 10 traders; offer escrow pilot |
| 37 farmers + 52 listings = liquidity | **Unproven** | Measure listings→trades; target one fully-cleared crop week |

Full register with kill-risks and tests: `RESEARCH-Assumptions-Register.md`.

## 4. The positioning shift (use this exact framing)

**Before (as pitched):** *"We connect farmers directly to buyers — no middlemen, no
commission."*
**Problem:** it is the same promise that has failed repeatedly, and it contradicts our own
slide notes.

**After (defensible):**
> "We don't remove the trader — we remove his **monopoly**. The farmer keeps the option to sell
> direct, sees the **real market price**, and can compare **multiple offers** instead of one
> broker's take-it-or-leave-it. Where the chain needs transport, storage, grading or credit —
> the things a website cannot do — we **partner with aggregators and make them verified and
> rated** instead of invisible. That is how we avoid the failure mode of the 40 projects before
> us."

This is also the honest pitch: our value is **choice + transparency + accountability**, not
magic disintermediation.

## 5. What we build next (P0) — and why it proves the thesis

| Feature | Replaces this middleman function | Proof it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Price Board** (asking/paid prices, farm-gate vs Mombasa) | Price discovery | Shows the hidden margin live |
| **Offers/Quotes on a listing** | "One buyer decides" | Farmers compare bids in the demo |
| **Mark as sold** | No trade data | Counts real trades, not listings |
| **Ratings / verified buyers** | Trust | Reputation accrues both ways |

Demand-led aggregation (RFQ → then bulk) and transport/collection registry follow (P1); escrow
with a finance partner is P2.

## 6. Honest risks we disclose (investors respect this)

1. Liquidity: a thin two-sided market can fail on timing, not headcount.
2. Farmer credit: removing the broker can remove working capital — needs a finance partner.
3. Device/literacy gap on the coast — field onboarding is the cost of adoption.
4. Subscription WTP is unproven — we will not bank the model on it.
5. Competitive: AgriVesta and TijaHub already offer RFQ/escrow/logistics `[S31]`; our edge is
   **coastal specificity + verified-aggregator accountability**.
