How to pay with M-Pesa

  • 1. Go to your M-Pesa and choose Send Money (or Lipa na M-Pesa).
  • 2. Send the plan amount to this M-Pesa number: 0745756470.
  • 3. Use the phone number you entered above as your payment reference.
  • Our team verifies your payment and activates your subscription immediately.
